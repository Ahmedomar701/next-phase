The Next Phase

Things are going to get hard from here. It’s not going to get easy.

However — we figured out one of the hardest problems in AI: getting these models to stop hallucinating. As a team, rolling this out and making sure it performs with enterprise-grade quality is one of the hardest things any company could ever do.

The bad news is we’re not there yet. The good news is we’re almost there.

We built this company from the ground up. It went $0 → $45M → $0 → $200M.

We want to make this company a $20 billion company by 2027.

This will not be easy. If it was easy, everybody would do it.

Where the focus goes

The entire focus of the company over the next couple of weeks:

  1. Get the platform stable.
  2. Invest energy and resources to fix it for good.
  3. Remove the integration bottleneck.

The leadership team is working day and night — hiring the right leaders, fixing the core fundamental problems, and getting the platform to where it needs to be.

It’s not going to be easy and we might lose some people along the way. But the journey to getting there will definitely be enjoyable. And when we get there, it’s going to pay off.

Platform stability

Where we were, and where we are. The bar plots the error rate rather than the stability figure, because at these magnitudes the interesting movement is in the residual, not the headline.[1]

Technical audio errors dropped from roughly 1,775 per week to 415 per week — a 4× reduction in four months.

AI generation stability

Generation errors fell from 13% to effectively zero. The model isn’t just better — it’s enterprise-ready.

Runway

We went out and secured capital from investors who don’t just write checks — they believe in what we’re building. LifeX. HP Ventures. Workday.

These aren’t passive investors. These are strategic partners who looked at our technology, our team, and our trajectory — and put real money behind it.

Runway went from six months to 1.1 years. That’s not just money — that’s time to execute. Time to get the platform right. Time to hire the right people. Time to win.

We’re not scrambling. We’re building — with the backing to see it through.

  1. A move from 95% to 99% looks like four points of improvement and is actually a five-fold reduction in failures. Always read the residual. ↩︎
