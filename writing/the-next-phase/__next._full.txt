10:T1688,<p>Things are going to get hard from here. It’s not going to get easy.</p>
<p>However — we figured out one of the hardest problems in AI: <strong>getting these models to stop hallucinating.</strong> As a team, rolling this out and making sure it performs with enterprise-grade quality is one of the hardest things any company could ever do.</p>
<p>The bad news is we’re not there yet. The good news is <strong>we’re almost there.</strong></p>
<hr>
<p>We built this company from the ground up. It went <code>$0</code> → <code>$45M</code> → <code>$0</code> → <code>$200M</code>.</p>
<blockquote>
<p>We want to make this company a $20 billion company by 2027.</p>
</blockquote>
<p>This will not be easy. If it was easy, everybody would do it.</p>
<h2 id="where-the-focus-goes">Where the focus goes</h2>
<p>The entire focus of the company over the next couple of weeks:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Get the platform stable.</strong></li>
<li><strong>Invest energy and resources to fix it for good.</strong></li>
<li><strong>Remove the integration bottleneck.</strong></li>
</ol>
<p>The leadership team is working day and night — hiring the right leaders, fixing the core fundamental problems, and getting the platform to where it needs to be.</p>
<p>It’s not going to be easy and we might lose some people along the way. But the journey to getting there will definitely be enjoyable. <strong>And when we get there, it’s going to pay off.</strong></p>
